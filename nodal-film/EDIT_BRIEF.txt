============================================
04. EDITING AND TRANSITIONS
============================================

Use the existing camera movement inside
the generated footage whenever possible.

Trim dead frames and repeated motion.

For transitions between clips:

- Prefer clean, invisible edits.
- Use matching movement and lighting.
- Use very short dissolves only when needed.
- Never obscure important architecture.
- Avoid duplicate frames at clip boundaries.

If adjacent clips share the same endpoint
and starting frame, trim to avoid an
unnecessary freeze or repeated movement.

Use subtle easing for any Remotion-generated
camera movement.

Never stretch footage unnaturally.

Preserve 16:9 framing without distortion.
