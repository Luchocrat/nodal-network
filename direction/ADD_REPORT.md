## Creative Thesis

`LOCKED` Make environmental accountability perceptible through the tension between speculative organic architecture and precise, inspectable evidence, expressed by a clear path from source to scenario to action, while refusing spectacle that implies certainty.

## Productive Tension

- Carrier force: scientific precision governs typography, alignment, units, controls and states.
- Expressive force: organic asymmetry governs architectural silhouettes, framing and network branches.
- Allowed tension: ivory structures within a severe grid; a compelling image paired with an exact evidence boundary.
- Remove tension from: button affordances, charts, body text and assessment conclusions. These must read immediately.
- Architecture supplies emotional impact; a visible ability to withhold deployment supplies trust.

## Signature Move

`LOCKED` Pair every compelling proposition with its evidence boundary in the same reading area.

- Dashboard: architectural view + CONCEPTUAL; NOAA value + station/time; balance + SIMULATED; suitability + UNKNOWN.
- Architecture caption: identifiable family/ID + “Concept visualization; unvalidated.”
- Presentation: show the operating result, then retain the unresolved site statement while describing the next study.
- Production rule: boundary is legible without hover or a footer visit; exact detail may expand.
- Failure: a reassuring result or image can be consumed before its scope is visible. Deleting this move turns the same vision into apparent endorsement.

## Forbidden Gravity

Plausible drift: cinematic reef imagery inside a generic glowing AI dashboard. The references encourage spectacular habitat visuals; dashboard conventions encourage status scores and busy HUDs. Symptoms: neon bloom, glass panels, small spaced capitals, invented trends, deployment-green badges, an authoritative AI answer dominating the evidence.

First correction: restore the source/scenario/action hierarchy, remove unsupported status, and increase meaningful labels. More decoration does not repair a broken evidence relationship.

Misleading decisions to prevent: node-local NOAA readings; unqualified “live”; biomass growth as prediction; board dimensions as specs; energy-green as ecological clearance; fabricated risk index; a Gemma response styled as observed evidence; missing data rendered as zero. Use adjacent scope labels, actual time/state, text/glyph semantics and explicit unavailable values instead. Transparency is a demonstrated interface behavior; environmental accountability is the intended purpose and still requires named reviewers, community participation and decision gates. Further review can support study, revision, comparison with alternatives or rejection; deployment is not the presumed outcome.
