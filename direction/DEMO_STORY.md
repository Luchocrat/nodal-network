## Evidence boundaries on screen

- **OBSERVED:** actual NOAA measurement, units, station `8723214`, source and observation time; fetched/cached/unavailable retrieval state is separate.
- **SIMULATED:** demand, generation, computed power balance, optional battery inputs. Label result “Illustrative power balance.”
- **CONCEPTUAL:** the network, coordinates, proposed hardware and habitat-oriented forms. No ecological growth prediction.
- **UNKNOWN:** site thermal/current/habitat/benthic evidence and permitting. Keep “Site ecological suitability: undetermined” visible in both energy states.

Use a named recommendation and its reason, not a made-up composite risk score. Do not call the NOAA panel “live at RIDGE.” Do not present tides as current speed, or source observations as thermal validation of computing infrastructure.

## Failure substitutions — continue the demo

| Failure | Show | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| NOAA fetch fails; genuine cached observation exists | Cached label with original observation time and source. | “NOAA retrieval is unavailable. This is a cached observation from the displayed time.” Continue scenario; do not advance its timestamp. |
| NOAA fetch fails; no verified cache | Clear unavailable panel; no numeric replacement. | “Regional observation retrieval is unavailable. NODAL exposes that missing evidence and still lets us inspect an explicit simulation.” |

The deterministic calculation and action remain inspectable; we are not presenting a generated response.” |
| Gemma response belongs to old inputs | Mark previous explanation stale or hide it until matching response arrives. | Show current deterministic assessment. Do not narrate an earlier response as interpretation of the −40 kW state. |

No API wait belongs on the critical presentation path. Verify a real model request before claiming a working Gemma integration or partner eligibility. Check the displayed response's numbers, scope and recommendation against the structured inputs; reject unsupported observations, thresholds or safety claims. A recorded successful response may be shown only if explicitly labeled with its matching inputs and run; a prepared paragraph is never labeled model output.

## One-minute Q&A

| Likely question | Direct answer |
|---|---|
| “Does NODAL prove underwater computing is safe?” | “No. It separates a reproducible energy scenario from unknown site suitability. Thermal, ecological, structural and permitting validation remain necessary.” |
| “What data is real?” | “The displayed NOAA observations are real when fetched or accurately cached. The station, time and source are exposed. They describe regional context, not proposed-node measurements.” |
| “What does the AI contribute?” | “It explains a deterministic assessment and its gaps from structured inputs. It does not calculate the authoritative result or certify deployment.” Use the actual integration state. |
| “What is the community impact?” | “The intended benefit is earlier inspection of assumptions and required evidence. Affected coastal communities and ocean users would need a role in defining acceptable impacts and reviewing proposals. No adoption or reduced environmental harm is measured today.” |
| “What would you build next?” | “Site-specific observations and validated thermal/habitat models with marine partners, plus named reviewers and decision gates that can compare alternatives or reject a proposal.” |

The close expresses the vision. Evidence labels demonstrate transparency today; accountable deployment also requires named reviewers, community participation and decision gates.
